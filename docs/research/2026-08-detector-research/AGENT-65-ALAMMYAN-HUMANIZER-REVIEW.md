## Methodology evolution

### Phase 1 — Detector-forward (2025–mid 2026)

- Screen 30+ tools from Google SERPs
- Per-tool: paste AI draft → humanize → screenshot detector panels
- Mix of **external** (Winston, GPTZero, Originality, Pangram, Quetext) and **built-in vendor panels**
- Heavy "bypass" framing in titles and H1s

### Phase 2 — Meaning-first (late 2026)

Documented in [How I Test AI Humanizers in 2026](https://www.anangsha.me/how-i-test-ai-humanizers-in-2026-my-meaning-first-framework/) and Feb 2026 Substack:

**Three fixed input types** (150–300 words each):

1. SEO blog paragraph  
2. Academic/explanatory paragraph  
3. Personal narrative paragraph  

**Six qualitative dimensions** (High/Medium/Low):

1. Meaning preservation  
2. Voice control  
3. Readability improvement  
4. Over-paraphrasing risk  
5. Factual drift  
6. Editing time to publishable  

**Explicit deprioritization:** "I stopped caring about detector scores almost entirely."

**Workflow rule:** Outline → draft → humanize → human edit (never humanize first).

**Tension:** SEO titles still say "bypass AI detectors" while body text and 2026 essays argue scores are unreliable. Likely **SEO intent vs. evolved editorial stance** — both are on the record.
